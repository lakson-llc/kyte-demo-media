# Kyte demo media

Copies of Blender Foundation open movies, used by the Demo provider in the Kyte app.
Each film is re-encoded to H.264 and AAC at up to 1280 pixels wide, with no other change.
The films are released under Creative Commons Attribution licences. They are shared here under those licences, with credit to their makers.
